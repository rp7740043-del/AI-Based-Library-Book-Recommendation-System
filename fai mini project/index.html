Foundations of AI Mini Project

In academic institutions, students often face choice overload when selecting follow-up textbooks matching their branch curriculum and active projects.

This system solves this challenge through a transparent hybrid pipeline: Content-Based Vector Space Models (VSM), Allotment Frequency Implicit Feedback, Curriculum Domain Graph Traversal, and Peer Collaborative Filtering.

[ Mathematical Foundation: Cosine Similarity of L2-Normalized Vectors ]
$$\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{d}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \cdot \|\mathbf{d}\|_2} = \sum_{k=1}^V \hat{u}_k \cdot \hat{d}_k$$

Where $\mathbf{u}$ represents the student profile vector (explicit interests + borrow frequency weights), and $\mathbf{d}$ represents the book feature vector (Category weight: 3.5, Tags: 2.5, Title: 1.0).

🧠

1. User Profiling & Topic Traversal

Encodes active curriculum interests combined with borrow frequency. Borrowing multiple books in a discipline (e.g. Python & Data Science) triggers semantic topic propagation to advanced domains like Machine Learning & Deep Learning.

🛡️

2. Strict Duplicate Prevention

The engine automatically audits the student's historical and active allotment registry. Books that have already been issued or returned are strictly excluded from recommendation candidates.

⭐

3. Academic Rating Weighting

Academic rating scores (out of 5.0) are normalized and combined with the base cosine distance, guaranteeing that seminal texts like Russell & Norvig's AI: A Modern Approach receive fair academic priority.

💡

4. Explainable AI (XAI) Reasons

Every recommendation card provides a clear, natural-language explanation tracing back to exact past borrowings or selected interests, eliminating black-box opacity.
